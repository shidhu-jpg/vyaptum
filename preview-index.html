Desktop 3D-Interactive Variants

Three different takes on adding 3D/interactive motion to the homepage. All are desktop-only — open each on a laptop/desktop browser and interact with the hero and cards. Mobile is completely unchanged in every variant.

Variant A

Mouse-Tilt Hero

The hero photo tilts in 3D and catches a light "shine" as your cursor moves over it — like a holographic card. Subtlest of the three, focused entirely on the hero.

Open Preview →
Variant B

Scroll Parallax Depth

Soft glowing color blobs and the hero image drift at different speeds as you scroll, creating a layered sense of depth through the hero section.

Open Preview →
Variant C

3D Hover Cards

Every card across Benefits, Documents Required and Why Choose Us tilts toward your cursor and lifts with a soft shadow on hover — interactive throughout the page, not just the hero.
