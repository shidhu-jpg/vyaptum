    Digital compliance

    Digital Signature Certificate (DSC)

    A DSC is an electronic signature used to authenticate documents and filings on approved government or business portals.

    Why it may be needed

    Depending on the service and the applicant's role, a DSC may be required for secure online signing and submission.

    What you may need

    PAN or other identity proof, address proof, email, mobile number and applicant details may be required for verification.

    How it works

    We confirm the appropriate certificate type, coordinate verification, support issuance and explain how it can be used for the relevant filing.

    DSC requirements vary by person, entity, role, portal and filing type. We confirm the current requirement before proceeding.
